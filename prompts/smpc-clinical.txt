Extract the clinical information actually present in this section:
- Therapeutic indications -> indications, with conditions, applicability,
  and eligible populations. Keep prevention and treatment settings distinct.
- Posology, dosage, administration -> dosage. Separate regimens by population,
  indication, route, and treatment phase. Retain adjustment thresholds,
  missed-dose instructions, preparation, and administration instructions.
- Explicit contraindications -> contraindications. Do not promote precautions
  into prohibitions. Retain hypersensitivity and concomitant-treatment details.
- Warnings and precautions -> warnings. Preserve conditions, applicability,
  populations, risk, recommendations, and monitoring when stated.
- Interactions -> interactions. Preserve named partners/classes, direction,
  affected substances or parameters, effects, consequences, and management.
- Fertility, pregnancy, lactation, driving, and machine use -> warnings when
  explicit precautions or risks are stated.
- Undesirable effects/adverse reactions -> adverse_reactions. Extract individual
  events with stated frequencies and populations, preserving table associations.
  Do not confuse frequency with severity or clinical seriousness. Preserve
  uncertain causal associations in the summary.
- Overdose -> overdose. Preserve reported manifestations, management or
  treatment, and information about reported cases when stated.
- Pharmacodynamic properties -> pharmacodynamics. Preserve the stated
  pharmacotherapeutic group, mechanism of action, effects, and efficacy/safety
  findings.
- Pharmacokinetic properties -> pharmacokinetics. Preserve absorption,
  distribution, metabolism, elimination, and properties in special populations.
- Preclinical safety data -> preclinical_safety. Preserve findings and any
  stated safety pharmacology, toxicology, genotoxicity, carcinogenicity, or
  reproductive/developmental toxicity details.
Nested Population and Indication instances also need a summary. Attach evidence
when available; otherwise omit it or use null.
Use null for absent optional attributes. Do not infer unstated associations.
In veterinary texts, follow headings and preserve the stated target species.
